# SEO Keyword Reference — Russian (RU) market

Source: Semrush organic position exports for two competitors, pulled 2026-08-10.
Use this when writing new page copy, city descriptions, meta tags, or blog articles.

- `meteo.kg` — the national met service. Ranks one page (`/ru/forecast`) for
  nearly everything, mostly positions 2–6.
- `sinoptik.ua` — a Ukrainian portal ranking on a single Bishkek page
  (`/ru/pohoda/bishkek`), mostly positions 6–13.

Both rank a **single generic forecast page** for the whole keyword set. Our site
has dedicated per-city pages, which is the structural advantage to press.

## What we changed (2026-08-11)

1. **Added the "Киргизия" spelling** — it was completely absent from the site
   despite ~1,360/mo. Now in the RU homepage and RU city descriptions.
2. **Proper Russian city names + prepositional case.** RU pages were rendering
   Kyrgyz-script names (`Өзгөн`, `Жалал-Абад`) and the title read
   "Погода в городе Бишкек". Now "Погода в Бишкеке", matching the 3,600/mo
   phrase exactly, with correct Russian forms per city.
3. **Time modifiers** — "на месяц", "на 10 дней", "на завтра" added to RU
   descriptions; "на неделю" and "почасовой" were already present.
4. **Both phrasings** — "погода" and "прогноз погоды" now both appear.

## Guidance for future copy

- Always include **both** country spellings where natural: Кыргызстан *and* Киргизия.
- Use the **prepositional** case for city mentions ("в Бишкеке", not "город Бишкек") —
  it matches how people actually search.
- Pair each city with at least one time modifier (сегодня / на завтра / на неделю /
  на месяц / на 10 дней).
- Keep `<title>` 50–60 chars and `meta description` 150–160 chars.
- Don't keyword-stuff: these are high-volume *informational* queries; Google
  rewards a page that actually answers them (live temps, hourly, 7/14-day).
- KG-language pages should target "аба ырайы <city>" — no competitor holds this well.
