Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How often is live weather data updated?
A: Pogoda Kg refreshes weather model data every 15 minutes automatically.

Q: Which Kyrgyzstan cities are supported?
A: All 10 major cities (Bishkek, Osh, Jalal-Abad, Karakol, Tokmok, Uzgen, Kara-Balta, Balykchy, Naryn, Talas) plus surrounding districts.

Q: Do you provide historical weather data?
A: Currently, we focus on real-time and 7-day forecast data. Historical data is not available.

Q: Is there a mobile app available?
A: Our website is fully mobile-responsive and works perfectly on all devices like a native app.

Q: How accurate is the 7-day forecast?
A: Our 7-day forecast uses advanced meteorological models specific to Kyrgyzstan's mountainous terrain for high accuracy.
