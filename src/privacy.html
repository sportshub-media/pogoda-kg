Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

1. Information We Collect

Pogoda Kg collects minimal data to provide weather forecast services. When you use our website, we may collect:

  • Your selected city and language preferences (stored locally in your browser)
  • Contact form submissions (name, email, location, message) when you voluntarily submit them
  • Basic usage analytics (page views, device type) via privacy-respecting analytics

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Provide and improve weather forecast services for Kyrgyzstan
  • Respond to your inquiries submitted through the contact form
  • Remember your language and city preferences for a better experience

3. Data Storage

Your preferences (language, recent searches) are stored in your browser's localStorage and are never transmitted to our servers. Contact form data is processed securely and used only for responding to your inquiry.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the Open-Meteo API for weather data, which does not track individual users. We use Cloudflare Pages for hosting, which may log basic request data (IP addresses) as part of standard web server operations.

5. Cookies

Pogoda Kg does not use tracking cookies. We use browser localStorage for your preferences only.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: info.pogoda.kg@gmail.com
